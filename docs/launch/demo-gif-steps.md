# Demo GIF — steps to record

Not recorded yet. Record against a locally served build (`npm run build`, then `npm run preview`)
using only the synthetic examples in `docs/tabletop/examples/`. Keep the browser address bar and
any personal bookmarks, profiles or notifications out of frame. Aim for 30–45 seconds at 360–1280px
width.

1. Open the intake page. Pause on the "No personal details, please" warning and the one-line
   "Rule 7 starts on …" banner.
2. Click **Open a saved file…** and choose `docs/tabletop/examples/scenario-1-processor-ransomware.json`.
3. Scroll the form briefly to show it filled with numbers and plain descriptions only.
4. In **Start the 72-hour clock from**, choose "When your vendor found out", then "When your
   organisation found out", to show the deadline change and its open legal question (AMB-13).
5. Open **Open legal questions** under "Is this a data breach?" to show the plain-language
   questions and their AMB references.
6. Switch the draft tabs with the arrow keys: To affected people → To the Board — first notice →
   To the Board — full report.
7. In the full report draft, scroll to a `[NOT PROVIDED]` placeholder, then to **Open legal
   questions for this draft** and the review checklist at the end.
8. Click **Download as a text file**, then **Save to file**.
9. End on the "Drafts only — not legal advice" badge.

Optional second take: open DevTools → Network before step 2 to show that only the page's own
files are requested.
