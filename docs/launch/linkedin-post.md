I have published dpdp-breach-kit, an open-source drafting aid for personal data breach
intimation under Rule 7 of India's DPDP Rules, 2025.

It turns the facts of an incident into three plain-text drafts: a notice to affected Data
Principals, an initial intimation to the Data Protection Board, and a detailed report for the
72-hour window. Facts you have not recorded stay visible as [NOT PROVIDED]; nothing is filled in
for you.

Where the Rule's text is ambiguous (for example, when "becoming aware" happens, or whether a
report at exactly 72 hours is in time), the tool flags the question and cites it. It does not
answer it for you. Those calls belong to you and your advisers.

The intake page runs locally in your browser. It makes no network calls and stores nothing; you
export the record yourself.

It also includes six fictional tabletop scenarios for practising the 72-hour window.

These are drafts only. This is not legal advice and not a compliance guarantee, and the Rule's
commencement and the Board's process should be checked before you rely on anything.

Feedback and corrections are welcome: https://github.com/PVUD14/dpdp-breach-kit
